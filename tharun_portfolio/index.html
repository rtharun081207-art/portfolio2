ReLoop 360 – Smart Circular Waste Exchange Platform

A smart waste management and exchange platform, built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26195), that turns unwanted materials into useful resources through reuse, recycling and donation.

Live demo ↗GitHub ↗

How it works

  • Modules for Industrial Exchange, LoopMarket, Exchange Offers and Donate & ReLoop.
  • Keyword-based smart matching connects buyers and sellers of materials.
  • Sign-up/login with JWT authentication and a request, accept or reject flow for orders.
